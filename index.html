The problem

You're on a roof, under a car, or with a client. Meanwhile a "Can you give me a quote?" email lands between receipts, newsletters and spam. By the time you see it, the customer may have hired someone who answered first.

Lead Catcher is built to make sure those emails get noticed, written down, and answered quickly, without you sitting at a desk.

How it works

  1. It spots the real leads. Quote requests and "can you come take a look?" emails get flagged. Spam, receipts and newsletters are skipped.
  2. It logs each one in your Google Sheet: name, contact info, what they need, where, how urgent, and the next step. The Sheet is in your own Google account.
  3. It drafts a reply right in the email thread. You read it, change anything you like, and press Send. Or you delete it.

Nothing is ever sent without your approval

  • Every reply waits as a draft until you press Send. If you never press Send, nothing goes out.
  • It never deletes, archives or moves your emails. It only adds a small label.
  • It never emails your contacts, posts anything, or pays for anything.
  • Danny never gets your Gmail password.

How your data is handled

  • It runs inside your own Google account as a small Google Apps Script. The lead Sheet belongs to you.
  • One outside service: to sort emails and write drafts, the text of new emails is sent to an AI service (Anthropic's Claude API, on a paid account). Anthropic's commercial terms say it does not train its AI models on your emails or the AI's replies. Anthropic keeps request data for a limited time for safety and legal reasons, then deletes it. Read Anthropic's data policy.
  • Danny only sees your lead Sheet if you share it with him for the weekly check-in. You can remove his access anytime.
  • When you stop, your Sheet stays yours, and Danny deletes any copies of your data he has.

When you first set it up, Google shows a "this app isn't verified" warning. That's normal for a private script made just for your account. It isn't a public app. On the setup call we read every permission together before you click Allow.

Questions owners ask

Will it email my customers by itself?

No. It only writes drafts. Nothing goes out until you open the draft and press Send.

Can Danny read my email?

No. The script runs in your Google account and I never get your password. I only see the lead Sheet if you choose to share it with me.

What if it gets something wrong?

It sometimes will. It might miss a lead, flag something that isn't one, or write a draft that needs editing. That's why you approve every reply. During the pilot I track every mistake and adjust it. At worst, a mistake costs you a deleted draft.

Will it mess up my inbox?

No. It doesn't delete, move or archive anything. It adds a label and leaves drafts in the thread. Remove it and your inbox is exactly as it was.

Does it work with Outlook or Yahoo?

Not yet. Right now it works with Gmail and Google Workspace only.

How long does setup take?

About 45 to 60 minutes on one video call. You click the buttons and I guide you. We test it with a fake lead before it goes live.

How do I cancel?

Anytime, with no fee. It takes about 2 minutes: turn off the script's trigger (it stops right away), then remove its access in your Google account settings. I'll walk you through it, and your Sheet stays yours.
